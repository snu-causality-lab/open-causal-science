# Lauritzen and Richardson (2002), causal interpretations of chain graphs

<!-- topic: Graphical Markov models -->

Steffen L. Lauritzen and Thomas S. Richardson, *Chain Graph Models and Their Causal Interpretations*, Journal of the Royal Statistical Society: Series B 64(3), 321–348. The authors interpret chain-graph distributions using equilibrium distributions of dynamic systems with feedback and use that interpretation to study interventions. [Published article](https://rss.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/1467-9868.00340)

The [finite chain-graph definitions](lauritzen-wermuth-1989-graphical-models.md) and [LWF probability statements](drton-2009-discrete-chain-graph-models.md) supply the graph and observational model for this connection. The dynamic construction, existence and convergence to equilibrium, and intervention semantics are open in Lean.
